How does the Buy 2, Get 2 FREE deal work? ▾
With the Yes! Program, you purchase a minimum of 4 windows and pay for 2 — Champion provides the other 2 at no cost. Alternatively, you can skip the program and take 40% off your total instead. Your in-home specialist will walk you through both options.
What does the free in-home estimate include? ▾
A Champion specialist visits your home, measures all your windows, helps you select styles and features, and gives you an exact price — all at no cost and no obligation to buy.
How long does installation take? ▾
Most installations are completed in a single day. Champion's expert technicians handle everything and perform a full cleanup before they leave.
What does the lifetime warranty cover? ▾
Champion's limited lifetime warranty covers the entire window frame, glass, and installation workmanship. It's one of the most comprehensive warranties in the industry and is transferable to a new owner if you sell your home.
Are Champion Windows really Energy Star certified? ▾
Yes. Champion's Comfort 365 glass features Low-E technology that reflects heat and blocks UV rays. Customers report average energy savings of 25–30% on their monthly bills.
Is financing available? ▾
Yes — Champion offers financing through the GreenSky program, with a 12-month promotional interest waiver period. Your specialist will review all payment options during your free estimate.
Is this offer available in my area? ▾
The offer availability is confirmed after you submit your zip code. Most markets qualify, but pricing and promotions vary. Claim your offer now to lock in the current deal before it expires.
