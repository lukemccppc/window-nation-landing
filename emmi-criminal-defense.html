Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I get in touch with Attorney Emmi?

You can call right now. Attorney Emmi is available for consultations and understands that criminal matters can't wait for business hours. The sooner you reach out, the sooner your defense begins.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes. Your initial consultation with Attorney Emmi is completely free and carries no obligation. You'll get honest guidance about your situation before making any decisions.

Should I talk to police before calling an attorney?

No. You have the constitutional right to remain silent and to have an attorney present during questioning. Politely invoke that right and contact Attorney Emmi before making any statements — even if you believe you're innocent.

What if I've already spoken to police?

It's not too late. Call Attorney Emmi immediately. He can review what was said, assess any potential issues, and develop a strategy to protect you going forward.

Can Attorney Emmi handle my case if I'm in Allegheny County?

Yes. Attorney Emmi defends clients throughout Allegheny County and the greater Pittsburgh area, including cases in municipal, magisterial district, and county courts.

What does it cost to hire a criminal defense attorney?

Fees vary depending on the charges and complexity of the case. Attorney Emmi will discuss fees transparently during your free consultation, with no surprise costs. The cost of quality defense is always less than the cost of a conviction.
