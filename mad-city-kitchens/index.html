A Fresh Kitchen Look. Without the Full Remodel.

Most of what makes a kitchen feel dated is on the surface. Refacing replaces those surfaces with new materials and keeps your existing cabinet boxes in place. You skip the demolition, plumbing reroutes, and extended downtime of a full remodel.

What’s new

  • New cabinet doors
  • New drawer fronts
  • Matching veneer on cabinet frames
  • Updated pulls and knobs

What stays

  • Your sturdy cabinet boxes
  • Your kitchen’s layout
  • Your plumbing, right where it is

Why Reface Instead of Replace?

If your cabinets are solid but tired-looking, replacing them means paying to tear out boxes that still work. Refacing puts your budget where you’ll see it.

  1. A Big Visual Change

    New doors, drawer fronts, and finishes change the look of the whole room, since cabinets cover more of your kitchen than anything else.

  2. Less Disruption

    Your existing cabinet boxes stay in place, so there’s no demolition and no plumbing reroutes, and the work finishes much faster than a full remodel.

  3. More Design Flexibility

    Mix and match door styles, colors, finishes, and hardware until it feels like your kitchen.

  4. A Smarter Way to Update

    Refresh the most visible part of your kitchen without the cost and upheaval of rebuilding it.
