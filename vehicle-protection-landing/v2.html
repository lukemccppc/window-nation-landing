Frequently Asked Questions

What does a vehicle protection plan cover?

Coverage varies by plan level, but most plans include major mechanical components like the engine, transmission, electrical system, and air conditioning — plus extras like 24/7 roadside assistance.

Can I use my own mechanic?

Yes. Your plan is accepted at your dealership or any ASE-certified repair shop nationwide — you're never limited to a specific network.

Is there a waiting period before I'm covered?

Most plans include a short waiting period before coverage begins, which helps keep costs low for every driver. Your specialist will walk you through the exact terms for your plan.

Will my monthly payment ever change?

No. Your monthly rate is locked in for the life of your contract, so you'll never be surprised by a rate increase.

Can I cancel my plan?

Yes, you can cancel anytime. If you cancel within the first 30 days, you'll receive a full refund under our money-back guarantee.

Does coverage work for older or high-mileage vehicles?

We offer plans for vehicles of varying ages and mileage. Get a free quote to see the coverage options available for your specific vehicle.
